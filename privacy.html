Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026

AR Storage Finder is designed to help you organize physical storage without turning information about your home or belongings into an online data source. The app works locally and does not require an account.

No data collection

AR Storage Finder does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It has no advertising, analytics, or tracking technology.

Stored on your iPhone

Storage areas, AR maps, markers, item details, tags, notes, quantities, and saved item photos remain in the app’s local storage on your device.

Camera processing stays local

Live camera frames are processed on-device with Apple frameworks for AR tracking and storage-surface detection. They are not uploaded.

Only photos you choose

The system photo picker gives the app only the image you select. A camera photo is saved only when you choose to add it to an item.

Information you create

You may create storage-area names, marker names, item names, quantities, tags, notes, AR world maps, and item photos. This content is used only to provide the app’s organization and search features on your device. Qijia Liu does not receive it.

Camera and photo access

The camera is required for augmented-reality tracking, on-device storage-surface detection, marker placement, and the optional capture of item photos. The app requests camera permission when you enter a feature that needs it. You can select an existing image through Apple’s system photo picker without giving the app broad access to your photo library.

External services and third parties

The app does not use third-party authentication, cloud storage, payment processing, advertising networks, analytics providers, AI services, or external data providers. Core functionality is delivered with Apple platform frameworks including SwiftData, ARKit, RealityKit, Vision, AVFoundation, and PhotosUI.

Data retention and deletion

Your content remains on the device until you delete an item, container, or storage area, remove a saved photo, or uninstall the app. Deleting a storage area also deletes its containers and items. The operating system may include local app data in a device backup according to Apple’s backup behavior and your settings; Qijia Liu does not receive that backup. Because there is no account or developer-operated server-side storage, there is no remote account or developer-held data to delete.

Children, regions, and protected content

AR Storage Finder is a general-purpose organization tool and is not directed to children. Its privacy behavior and functionality are the same in every region. It does not provide regulated services or protected third-party content.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the revised version is released.

Contact
