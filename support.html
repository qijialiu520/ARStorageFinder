Support

Setup, troubleshooting, and contact information

AR Storage Finder works without a login. Everything you need is in the app on your iPhone.

1

Create a space

On the Spaces tab, tap + and name a closet, garage, pantry, or other area.

2

Scan and mark

Allow camera access, move your iPhone slowly, then tap a highlighted storage surface or add one manually.

3

Add items

Open a marker and save item names, photos, quantities, tags, and notes.

4

Find anything

Use the Find tab to search, filter by tag, and return to an item’s marker in AR.

Common questions

The camera or AR view is unavailable

Use an ARKit-capable iPhone running iOS 18.5 or later. In Settings, open Privacy & Security → Camera and enable access for AR Storage Finder. Restart the app after changing access.

A saved marker does not line up

Return to the original area, point toward the view used during setup, and move slowly while AR relocalizes. Good lighting and textured surfaces improve tracking.

An item is missing from search

Search checks item names, notes, and tags. Clear an active tag filter, then search with a shorter word. Confirm the item is still linked to its container.

How is my library backed up?

The app does not run its own cloud service or sync. Its local data may be included in an iPhone backup according to Apple’s backup behavior and your settings. Qijia Liu does not receive that backup. Uninstalling the app removes its local data.

Compatibility

Requires an ARKit-capable iPhone with iOS 18.5 or later. The core AR workflow requires a physical iPhone and is not available in the iOS Simulator.

Still need help?

Email kickhard.kicksmart@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Do not include private photos or a copy of your storage library.
